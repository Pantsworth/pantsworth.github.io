Hi, I'm Michael

I'm a Senior Software Engineer in Apple's Vision Products Group in Seattle WA, where I build the tools, pipelines, and developer platforms that help artists and engineers make 3D content for AR/VR products like the Apple Vision Pro.

Before Apple, I was a Lead Technical Director at LAIKA in Portland, Oregon, an Oscar-nominated, Golden Globe-winning stopmotion animation company. I worked with folks all over the studio to maintain and develop workflows related to scheduling, fabrication, and work tracking.
